A curated pre-TOKEN2049 gathering of founders, VCs, investors and operators building the next generation of Web3. 8Blocks co-hosted the night.

Why we were there
Tokenization puts ownership on chain. It doesn't put a price on it, or a reason to hold.

Right room, right night. On October 6, before the TOKEN2049 main stage opened, we helped bring together the founders and the capital actually building the next cycle. RWA is where 8Blocks is putting its weight now: designing the economics that make a tokenized real-world asset worth holding, not just technically on-chain.

Who we can help, and with what
  • Founders building or raising in RWA and tokenomics who want the economics right before the market tests them.
  • Allocators who want a straight read on whether a token model actually works.
  • Teams with a live token whose demand comes from incentives rather than from the product.
